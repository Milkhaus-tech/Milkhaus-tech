<h1>MILKHAUS</h1>

Hey, I'm **Noah**. I do live production in Las Vegas (AV, lighting, video walls, the
whole signal chain) and I've been doing it since 2017. Milkhaus is what I call my
homelab: a few servers at home hosting game servers for my friends, a movie site, my
Discord bots, and way too much automation for my house.

### Stuff worth looking at

- **[homelab](https://github.com/Milkhaus-tech/homelab)** is the whole setup: the real
  configs and scripts, passwords pulled out, plus write-ups for every time I broke
  something, which is a lot lol.
- **[milkhaus.net](https://milkhaus.net)** has what's running right now and how to get in.

### Other stuff I've built

- **Teto** is my Discord bot. She chats, remembers people, does text-to-speech, and runs
  a few games with free chips. Code's private, but you can
  [add her](https://milkhaus.net/teto), and her policies are in
  [teto-zone](https://github.com/Milkhaus-tech/teto-zone).
- **A fantasy football assistant** for my leagues that only makes the moves it's
  confident about. Private, happy to walk through it.
- **Instagram and X link fixers** so posts actually preview in Discord:
  [igembed](https://github.com/Milkhaus-tech/homelab/tree/main/services/igembed) and
  [xembed](https://github.com/Milkhaus-tech/homelab/tree/main/services/xembed).
- **[eraser](https://github.com/Milkhaus-tech/eraser)**, my fork of a data-broker
  opt-out tool that runs monthly on GitHub Actions.
